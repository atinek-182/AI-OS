## How to Win an Awwward

Have you ever wondered what goes into making an award-winning website? The combination of visual creativity and technical craft that is displayed on Awwwards.com is fascinating to me. As I got more and more into web development myself, my appreciation for the creativity of the featured studios and independent creatives has only grown. It’s my go-to location to find inspiration for web development, and I have watched in awe over the last decade to find out which creations would emerge next.

Let me preface the following article with a disclaimer. I am not an Awwward-winning designer or developer (yet). I have yet to submit a website on the Awwwards-platform. With 2021 now more than three months underway, this article is mostly meant as an anchor for myself; a reminder to reach my goal to win an Awwward this year. I hope you find some value in its contents.

In the following paragraphs I’d like to share some of the elements that constitute an engaging digital experience. Be warned, UX purists might be offended by some of the designs on display.
